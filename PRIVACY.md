# Tabiato 旅跡 — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Tabiato is a travel-memory app built by team GroupName for the CodeNection 2026 hackathon.
This page explains what the app stores and who can see it.

## What we store
- **Your account:** email address, display name, username and profile photo. If you sign in
  with Google, we receive only your name, email and profile picture.
- **What you add to a trip:** photos, the time and location read from each photo, places,
  ratings, spending, notes, comments and messages.

## Who can see it
- A trip is private to the people on it unless you make it public.
- On a public trip, you choose whether photos, ratings and comments are shown. Notes and
  individual expenses are never public; spending is shown only as category totals, and only
  if you switch it on.
- Access rules are enforced in the database, not only in the app.

## Services we use
- **Supabase** (Singapore) stores your account, trip data and photos.
- **Vercel** hosts the app and records anonymous page-view statistics.
- **Groq**, through our **n8n** workflow, writes the memory book and other text from numbers we
  calculate from your trip.
- **OpenStreetMap services** (Nominatim, Overpass), **Open-Meteo**, **GDACS**, **GOV.UK** and
  **NewsData.io** receive place names or coordinates to return place names, weather, alerts
  and travel news. They do not receive your account details.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

## Deleting your data
You can delete any trip you own from Trip options; its photos are deleted with it. To delete
your whole account, email us at the address below.
